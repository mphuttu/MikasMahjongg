Rules and tile matching

Clear the board by removing matching pairs. A tile is free when no tile in any higher layer covers it and at least one of its left or right sides is open. Tiles may only be removed when both are free.

Bamboo, circle, character, wind, and dragon tiles match identical faces. Any flower matches any other flower; any season matches any other season. Flowers do not match seasons.

Same-layer tile faces touch; exposed sides and the offsets of upper layers help distinguish tiles that overlap. The counter in the status bar reports how many tiles remain.

Tip: Prefer moves that uncover tiles or open a blocked side. Use undo if a choice closes off useful pairs.
